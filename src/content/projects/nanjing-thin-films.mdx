A summer in the Solid State Microstructures Lab at Nanjing University, learning how the
building blocks of electronic devices are actually made — atom layer by atom layer.

## Hands-on work

- Applied **physical vapour deposition (PVD)** and **spin-coating** to fabricate
  **Fe₃O₄ thin-film substrates**.
- Operated **photolithography** for circuit patterning.

## Self-study

Alongside the fabrication, I taught myself the principles of **Scanning Tunnelling
Microscopy (STM)** and vacuum-gauge operation — including STM's role in controlling 2D
crystal growth and in atomic-scale data storage. It was my first real exposure to the
characterisation tools I now use in materials research.
