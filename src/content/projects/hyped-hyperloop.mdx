HYPED is the University of Edinburgh's student Hyperloop team, building toward faster,
lower-carbon transport. I joined as **Shell Engineer** on the team that produced the
UK's first student-built Hyperloop test pod.

## My role

- Owned the **aerodynamic design and manufacturing of the shell** — the pod's outer
  aeroshell — inside a cross-functional student team.
- Selected a **fibreglass composite** layup that cut shell cost by roughly **20%**,
  a deliberate trade-off of performance against a student budget.
- Worked hands-on through fabrication: mould prep, layup, finishing and fitting.

## Why it mattered

Beyond the engineering, HYPED was my first taste of a real multidisciplinary build —
mechanical, electronics and software teams converging on a single deadline. It's where
I learned that a good decision is usually the one that survives contact with a budget.
