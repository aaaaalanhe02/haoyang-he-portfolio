A self-directed analytics project to practise turning raw transaction data into
commercial decisions.

## The work

- Analysed **10,000+ transaction records** in SQL — using **CTEs, window functions and
  aggregations** — to evaluate **year-over-year revenue growth, product performance and
  seasonality**.
- Delivered **ABC (Pareto)** and **RFM** segmentation to identify high-value products and
  customer cohorts.

## The point

The interesting part wasn't the queries, it was the framing: which 20% of products carry
80% of revenue, which customers are quietly churning, and what an inventory or retention
team should actually *do* about it. It's the same evidence-first instinct I bring to lab
work, pointed at a spreadsheet instead of a rheometer.
