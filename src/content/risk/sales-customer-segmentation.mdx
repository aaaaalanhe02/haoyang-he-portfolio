A self-directed analytics project: take a raw transactional dataset and turn it into
decisions that a commercial team could act on.

## What I did

- Queried **10,000+ transaction records** in SQL — **CTEs, window functions and
  aggregations** — to track **year-on-year revenue, product performance and seasonality**,
  identifying the trends behind shifts in demand.
- Segmented the customer portfolio with **RFM** (recency, frequency, monetary value) and
  **Pareto** analysis to rank value and **concentration**, flagging high-value cohorts for
  retention.
- Turned the findings into concise, **management-information-style** insight to support
  revenue, inventory and retention decisions.

## Risk lens

Swap customers for borrowers and revenue for exposure, and the same toolkit answers
portfolio-monitoring questions: which cohorts are growing or deteriorating, where exposure
is concentrated, and which movements are seasonal rather than structural. A window function
that computes year-on-year revenue computes month-on-month arrears just as easily.
