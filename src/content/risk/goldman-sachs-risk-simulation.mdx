A practical job simulation, completed on Forage, built around the work of Goldman Sachs'
Risk team. It covered two tasks: **an introduction to risk**, and **evaluating client
profiles and real estate investments**.

## What I did

- **Credit risk assessment.** Evaluated individual client profiles from quantified
  financial data — credit score, income, debt, savings and payment history — scoring each
  one with a weighted, normalised model and banding it as low, medium or high risk.
- **Real estate scenario analysis.** Applied quantitative analysis to the risks facing an
  investment property — rising property taxes, a major repair bill, falling occupancy, a
  market downturn and new competing developments — rating each by probability and
  interpreting its financial implications.
- **Risk mitigation.** Recommended a proportionate mitigation strategy for each risk given
  its level, and justified the choice against the alternatives.

## Risk lens

This is the core loop of a risk role in miniature: turn information about a client into a
consistent, quantitative view; set thresholds that separate acceptable from unacceptable
risk; then recommend action in proportion to the risk. The same structure scales from a
single borrower to a whole lending portfolio.
