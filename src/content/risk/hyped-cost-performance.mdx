HYPED is the University of Edinburgh's student Hyperloop team. I joined as **Shell
Engineer** on the team that built the UK's first student-built Hyperloop test pod.

## What I did

- Owned the **aerodynamic design and manufacture of the pod shell**, aligning design
  decisions with cross-functional team members across mechanical, electronics and software.
- **Weighed cost against performance** by choosing fibreglass over carbon fibre, cutting
  shell cost by **about 20%** while maintaining test performance.

## Risk lens

On a student budget, every engineering decision is a risk–return decision: what do we give
up, what could go wrong, and is the saving worth it? Making that call with evidence, and
bringing a cross-functional team along with it, is the same judgement a risk function
exercises when it weighs growth against expected loss.
